For publishers

Turn unsold weeks into a market. List inventory, set a price for any week, and let buyers compete for it.

  • List any week, day or ad break and set your own price, reserve and auto-accept.
  • Decide on every winning bid within 24 hours; see the advertiser before you accept.
  • Get paid 3 business days after the buyer approves the proof.
  • Nothing to pay up front: a fee per deal, agreed with Nashr, and no subscription.
Become a media partner

How a booking is paid

  1. 1Bid or book. Nothing is charged when you bid or when you win.
  2. 2The publisher accepts within 24 hours. You are never charged automatically.
  3. 3You confirm payment within 48 hours. The deal shows Secured.
  4. 4Held by Stripe through Nashr until the placement is verified. Nashr never holds your funds.
  5. 5Released when you approve the proof, or after 5 working days. The publisher is paid out 3 business days later.
