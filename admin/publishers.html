    How the fee works

    Range
    10 to 15% of each sale, set per publisher by Nashr admin
    Default
    15% for a new publisher
    VAT
    charged on the fee only: 5% in the UAE, 15% in KSA
    Who pays
    the publisher, out of each sale; buyers never see a fee
    Invoices
    Nashr invoices the publisher for its fee; the publisher invoices the buyer for the media Proposed
    Changes
    made only by Nashr admin and written to the audit log
