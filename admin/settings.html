    Fees and bidding

    Each publisher's own fee, 10 to 15%, is set on Publishers. The fee is charged to the publisher; buyers never see it.

    Rounded up to the next 100: a top bid of AED 67,800 needs at least AED 69,900. Proposed: a fixed amount instead of a percentage Proposed.
