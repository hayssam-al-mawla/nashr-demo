    How these figures are counted

    • Gross transaction value, commission, take rate and net take match Operations and Funds for every market and channel since February. Payments are held by Stripe through Nashr until the placement is verified; Nashr never holds the funds.
    • SAR is shown in AED at 1 SAR = 0.979 AED, for reporting only. AED and SAR are held and paid out separately.
    • Deals are the deals accepted, as on Publishers: payments, plus the two waiting for the buyer to pay.
    • Nothing here is charged or changed. Downloads are CSV files made in your browser from the tables on this page.
