    How a listing is checked

    1. 1Price: against the outlet's rate card and comparable slots on Nashr. Flagged when more than 30% from the middle Proposed
    2. 2Specs: size, file and the proof the buyer gets, per channel.
    3. 3Schedule: clashes with public holidays, blackouts and weeks already booked.
    4. 4Nashr approves, sends it back with a reason or asks a question. Nashr never edits a price.
    5. 5The publisher is told by email and every decision is written to the audit log. Proposed: Nashr answers within 1 working day Proposed

    Content standards Proposed

    Gambling
    Not allowed. A prize draw runs only with a permit from the commerce authority, shown in the ad. Looks for betting, casinos, lotteries, prize draws.
    Tobacco
    Not allowed, including vapes and heated tobacco. Looks for cigarettes, vapes, shisha.
    Political
    Not allowed: parties, candidates, campaigns and causes. Looks for elections, parties, campaigns.
    Alcohol
    Not allowed in KSA. In the UAE only where the publisher allows it. Looks for drinks, bars and brands.
    Financial promotions
    Allowed when the ad shows the regulator's licence line. Looks for loans, cards, investments, crypto trading.
    Health claims
    Allowed with the approval number from the health authority. Looks for cures, weight loss, supplements.

    Each publisher can decline more. Gulf News declines gambling, tobacco and political ads. The check reads the file, the brief and the advertiser's category.
