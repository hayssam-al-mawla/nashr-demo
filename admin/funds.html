    Currencies

    How money moves

    1. 1The publisher accepts: Awaiting buyer payment. Nothing is held and nothing is charged until the buyer confirms.
    2. 2The buyer confirms payment: Secured, held by Stripe through Nashr.
    3. 3The publisher submits proof: In buyer review, for 5 working days.
    4. 4The buyer approves, or the review window ends: Released.
    5. 5Paid out to the publisher 3 business days later, after the Nashr fee and VAT on the fee.
    6. 6Refunded, in full or in part, when a deal is cancelled, the proof deadline is missed or a dispute is settled for the buyer.
